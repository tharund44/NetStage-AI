# Topology Documentation

R1 has two paths to the data centre: a GigabitEthernet link (Gi0/1 = 10.10.50.1/30 to R3 Gi0/0 = 10.10.50.2/30) and a slow serial WAN (S0/0/0 = 10.10.99.1/30 to R2). R2 eventually reaches R3 through R4 and R5.
