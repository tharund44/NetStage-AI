# Topology Documentation

AP1 (Cisco Lightweight AP or autonomous AP in Packet Tracer) broadcasts two SSIDs: 'Corporate-WiFi' mapped to VLAN 10 and 'Guest-WiFi'. AP1 uplink on SW1 Fa0/24 is a trunk. VLAN 10 = Corporate (10.10.10.0/24), VLAN 99 = Guest (10.10.99.0/24).

*Note: Please consult the .pkt file for exact device names and interfaces.*
